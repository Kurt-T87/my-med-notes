## 📄 Abstract
Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) and sacituzumab govitecan (SG) are both approved for metastatic breast cancer (mBC), and patients with HER2-low disease are often candidates for both, but the optimal sequence is undefined. This systematic review and meta-analysis compared outcomes of the second antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) by treatment sequence (SG→T-DXd versus T-DXd→SG).
Following PRISMA (PROSPERO CRD420251250322), PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Cochrane CENTRAL and major oncology conference proceedings were searched through December 2025 for studies of sequential T-DXd and SG in mBC. The primary endpoint was progression-free survival on the second ADC (PFS2). Hazard ratios (HRs) were obtained from patient-level data, published estimates or reconstructed Kaplan-Meier curves and pooled using a random-effects model. Study heterogeneity was assessed using the I² statistic. Risk of bias and certainty of evidence were assessed with ROBINS-I and GRADE.
Of 694 records, 17 retrospective studies were included; seven (292 SG→T-DXd and 286 T-DXd→SG patients) contributed to the PFS2 meta-analysis. The pooled HR for T-DXd→SG versus SG→T-DXd was 1.54 (95% CI 0.98-2.40; I²=61%), 1.72 (95% CI 1.23-2.41; I²=10%) after excluding one study, and 1.36 (95% CI 0.89-2.10) with covariate-adjusted estimates. Median PFS2 was 3.3 months with SG→T-DXd and 2.8 months with T-DXd→SG.
Point estimates favoured SG→T-DXd, but given very low certainty and likely confounding by tumour subtype, current data do not establish the superiority of either sequence. Prospective, subtype-stratified studies are needed.
